# VOICE mode quick start (Lenticular mode 4)

Half the knobs in VOICE are contextual — they do nothing until
something else is on. Patch in this order.

## 1. Get there
Mode button to the **4th mode** (where spectral used to live).
Blend page 1 → **full wet** — dry bleed makes VOICE sound broken
(you hear yourself doubled instead of transformed).

## 2. Make it track (this gates everything)
One clear, pitched, **monophonic** source — bass or voice, no chords,
no heavy FX in front. IN GAIN so the meter dances in the middle.
PITCH noon, TEXTURE ~9:00, DENSITY noon.

Play a note: you should hear *you*, slightly "held" — the wavetable
tracking you. If it's unpitched chatter instead, the tracker isn't
locking: adjust IN GAIN, play cleaner single notes.

## 3. Controls, in the order they come alive
- **PITCH / V-Oct** — transpose without chipmunking. Works solo.
- **Blend page 4** (where reverb was): the chord. Turn up *slowly* —
  just past the bottom is octave, then fifth, sus4, minor, m7…
  Unlike granular (statistics pick one tone per grain), in VOICE
  **all four chord tones sound at once** — the knob position *is*
  the chord. m7 sits a bit below halfway.
- **SIZE** — dead until Harmony is on; then choir looseness
  (up to ±30 cents of humanize per voice).
- **Blend page 2** — also needs Harmony on: fans the choir in stereo.
- **TEXTURE** — the throat: left = machine-flat, 9:00–noon = human,
  hard right = vocal fry. Subtle except at the extremes.
- **POSITION** — dead live; works **after FREEZE**: freeze a held
  vowel, POSITION scrubs the captured cycles, V/Oct plays the frozen
  vowel at any pitch.

## The 60-second choir
Full wet → sing a steady "ahh" → page 4 to ~10 o'clock (m7) →
page 2 up → SIZE noon. Four of you, in stereo.

## Remember
- Consonants pass through untransposed on purpose — s's stay s's.
- Bass tracks down to 50 Hz; watch drop-D fundamentals.
- Up-shift caps around +19 semitones.
